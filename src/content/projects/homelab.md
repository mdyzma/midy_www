My homelab brings infrastructure and configuration together in code. Terraform provisions resources in Proxmox VE, while Ansible configures and hardens the physical host. Automation scripts support day-to-day management.

The repository documents the hardware, ZFS storage, network design, and service inventory, along with a disaster recovery guide for rebuilding the environment from scratch.
